About this dataset

The Joint Actuator Explorer is an open, continuously maintained collection of specifications for actuators used in legged robots — quadrupeds, bipeds, and humanoids. Entries are sourced from manufacturer datasheets and published research. Contributions are welcome on GitHub: edit data/actuators.json following the schema in data/schema.md and open a pull request.
